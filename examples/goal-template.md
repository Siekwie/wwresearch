Find the smallest counterexample to <conjecture>, or prove that none exists

## Statement

State the problem exactly. Define every symbol. If there is a standard formulation, quote it.

## What counts as solved

- A complete proof, **or**
- an explicit counterexample together with a script that verifies it in under a minute.

Partial progress worth reporting: a proof for a special case, a reduction to a known
problem, a computational bound, a reason why a natural approach cannot work.

## What is already known

- Known results and where they stop (with references, if you have them).
- Approaches that are known to fail, and why. Agents will not repeat them.

## Constraints

- Tools, languages or libraries agents should use (or avoid).
- Files in this repository that are relevant: `data/…`, `src/…`.
- Anything that is out of scope.
